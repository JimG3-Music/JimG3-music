The Story

JimG3 is a songwriter and musician from Airdrie, Scotland, and a dad of three girls.

This project is about completing a goal set for his family and creating a collection of songs that can be remembered.

Every recording is made at home in Airdrie.

About JimG3

Members of the ML6 Community Music Group may join JimG3 on selected recordings and performances.
