My Story

I have always believed music is about more than simply recording songs. For me, it is about creating something honest that can be shared, remembered and passed on.

I write and record my music at home in Airdrie, Scotland. Every song has its own story, and I want this site to be a place where I can share those stories as well as the music.

About Me

I'm a dad of three girls, a songwriter and a musician.

At 35, I became a heart-attack survivor. I'm disabled, and life has changed in ways I never expected, but music has remained one of the things I love most.

I play multiple instruments while I still can. I write, record and make music because I want to keep doing what I love and share it with other people.

These songs are my way of telling stories, making memories and putting something of myself into the world.

I'm also connected with the ML6 Community Music Group in Airdrie.
