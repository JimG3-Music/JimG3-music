The Story

Jim is a songwriter and musician from Airdrie, Scotland, and a dad of three girls.

Music has always been about more than simply recording songs. It's about creating something that can be shared, remembered and passed on.

Every recording is made at home in Airdrie.

About Jim

PHOTO COMING SOON

Dad of three girls. Songwriter. Musician.

At just 35, Jim became a heart attack survivor. Life changed, but music remained one of the things that mattered most.

Now Jim is sharing what he loves most: writing songs, making music and telling stories through it.

All songs are written and performed by Jim unless otherwise stated.

Jim is also connected with the ML6 Community Music Group in Airdrie.
